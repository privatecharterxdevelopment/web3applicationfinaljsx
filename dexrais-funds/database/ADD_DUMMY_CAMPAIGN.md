# Adding the Dummy Campaign

To add the dummy campaign to your Supabase database:

1. Go to your Supabase project dashboard: https://supabase.com/dashboard/project/ttzinesrosreceefzitz

2. Click on "SQL Editor" in the left sidebar

3. Click "New Query"

4. Copy and paste the contents of `database/dummy-campaign.sql`

5. Click "Run" to execute the query

This will create a sample DeFi campaign that you can view at:
`http://localhost:5174/campaign/c1e5a8b0-1234-4567-89ab-123456789abc`
